# Character Blueprint - Phase 1

The current goal is **not** animation or livestreaming yet.

The project focus is to create and approve a photorealistic 3D human character first.
Only after the look is approved should the workflow continue with rigging, motion retargeting, and final rendering.

## Current default concept

- **Codename:** Nova
- **Gender presentation:** Female
- **Apparent age:** 22-25
- **Style target:** Photorealistic digital human
- **Hair:** Long black hair
- **Face goal:** Young, elegant, modern, believable
- **Body goal:** Real-world proportions, suitable for dancing
- **Outfit direction:** Premium modern outfit that works for TikTok performance
- **Use case:** A reusable realistic dance avatar for later retargeted performances

## Files to prepare later

- `models/characters/main_character.glb` - web preview asset
- `models/characters/main_character.blend` or `.fbx` - source production asset

## Review checklist

- Face looks believable from front, side, and 3/4 view
- Eyes, teeth, skin, and hair feel realistic
- Body proportions are natural
- Outfit looks premium and plausible
- Character feels suitable for short-form video content
- Approved before any dance animation work begins
